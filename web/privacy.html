Sumate: privacy

Last updated: 18 November 2026. Sumate is free, open-source sign-up sheets by Juris Games (source code). The Android app opens the same web app at signup.jurisgames.com.

What we collect

No accounts, no ads, no analytics and no trackers. We never ask for an email or a phone number. Whatever you type into a field (a title, a name, a note) is sent as you typed it, so an email address or phone number typed there is sent too.

Sheets and sign-ups

  • A sheet (its title, details and lines) is kept on our server so everyone with the link can see it.
  • When you sign up, the name and the optional note you type are kept with the sheet. Anyone with the sheet's link sees them, unless the organizer chose to show names only to the organizer.
  • Your device keeps your name, a random code that lets you remove your own sign-ups, and the list of sheets you made or joined. It is not linked to who you are.
  • You can remove your own sign-up at any time. The organizer can remove any sign-up or delete the whole sheet, which deletes it from the server at once. A sheet nobody changes for 90 days is deleted automatically.

Delete your data

  1. Your sign-up (name and note): open the sheet in Sumate and tap the × on your name. It is deleted from the server at once. Only the device you signed up from can do this; from another device, ask the organizer.
  2. A whole sheet with everyone's sign-ups: the organizer opens it, taps Edit, then "Delete this sheet" twice. Everything in it is deleted from the server at once.
  3. On your device: clearing the site's data (or uninstalling the app) deletes your saved name and list of sheets.
  4. Anything left is deleted automatically 90 days after the sheet's last change. Nothing is kept after that, and there are no backups of sheets. Help: hello@jurisgames.com

The server

Sumate runs on Cloudflare Workers. Cloudflare may keep standard security logs.

Children

Sumate is a general-audience tool and is not directed at children.

Sumate: privacidad

Sumate es una app gratis y de código abierto para hacer listas donde la gente se anota. Sin cuentas, sin anuncios, sin analíticas; nunca pedimos email ni teléfono. Lo que escribís en un campo (un título, un nombre, una nota) se envía tal cual, así que si escribís ahí un email o un teléfono también se envía. El servidor guarda la lista y los nombres y notas que cada uno escribe al anotarse; los ve cualquiera con el enlace, salvo que quien organiza elija mostrarlos solo a sí mismo. Podés sacarte de una lista cuando quieras; quien organiza puede quitar a cualquiera o borrar la lista, y se borra del servidor en el momento. Para borrar tu nombre: abrí la lista y tocá la × al lado de tu nombre. Para borrar toda la lista: quien organiza toca Editar y después "Borrar esta lista" dos veces. Una lista que nadie cambia en 90 días se borra sola. Contacto: hello@jurisgames.com
