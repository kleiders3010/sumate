Sumate: privacy

Last updated: 18 November 2026. Sumate is free, open-source sign-up sheets by Juris Games (source code). The Android app opens the same web app at signup.jurisgames.com.

What we collect

No accounts, no ads, no analytics and no trackers. We never ask for an email or a phone number, except the optional reply address in "Send feedback". Whatever you type into a field (a title, a name, a note) is sent as you typed it, so an email address or phone number typed there is sent too.

Sheets and sign-ups

  • A sheet (its title, details and lines) is kept on our server so everyone with the link can see it.
  • When you sign up, the name and the optional note you type are kept with the sheet. Anyone with the sheet's link sees them, unless the organizer chose to show names only to the organizer.
  • Your device keeps your name, a random code that lets you remove your own sign-ups, and the list of sheets you made or joined. It is not linked to who you are.
  • You can remove your own sign-up at any time. The organizer can remove any sign-up or delete the whole sheet, which deletes it from the server at once. A sheet nobody changes for 90 days is deleted automatically.

Delete your data

  1. Your sign-up (name and note): open the sheet in Sumate and tap the × on your name. It is deleted from the server at once. Only the device you signed up from can do this; from another device, ask the organizer.
  2. A whole sheet with everyone's sign-ups: the organizer opens it, taps Edit, then "Delete this sheet" twice. Everything in it is deleted from the server at once.
  3. On your device: clearing the site's data (or uninstalling the app) deletes your saved name and list of sheets.
  4. Anything left is deleted automatically 90 days after the sheet's last change. Nothing is kept after that, and there are no backups of sheets. Help: hello@jurisgames.com

The server

Sumate runs on Cloudflare Workers. Cloudflare may keep standard security logs.

Feedback

"Send feedback" sends what you write, its kind (a problem, an idea, something else) and, only if you type it, your email, to feedback.jurisgames.com, which emails it to hello@jurisgames.com and stores nothing itself. With "Include technical details" (ticked at first; untick it to leave them out, and "See what's sent" shows everything before it goes) it also sends your browser and system, screen and window size, language, connection type, recent error messages of this page and which page you are on. Never the sheet, its links or anyone's name. The email stays in our inbox as long as it takes to answer and fix things. To stop floods the server counts reports per network per day under a one-way code of the network address (never the address itself), deleted the next day.

Children

Sumate is a general-audience tool and is not directed at children.

Sumate: privacidad

Sumate es una app gratis y de código abierto para hacer listas donde la gente se anota. Sin cuentas, sin anuncios, sin analíticas; nunca pedimos email ni teléfono, salvo el email opcional para responderte en "Enviar comentarios". Lo que escribís en un campo (un título, un nombre, una nota) se envía tal cual, así que si escribís ahí un email o un teléfono también se envía. El servidor guarda la lista y los nombres y notas que cada uno escribe al anotarse; los ve cualquiera con el enlace, salvo que quien organiza elija mostrarlos solo a sí mismo. Podés sacarte de una lista cuando quieras; quien organiza puede quitar a cualquiera o borrar la lista, y se borra del servidor en el momento. Para borrar tu nombre: abrí la lista y tocá la × al lado de tu nombre. Para borrar toda la lista: quien organiza toca Editar y después "Borrar esta lista" dos veces. Una lista que nadie cambia en 90 días se borra sola. "Enviar comentarios" manda lo que escribís, de qué tipo es (un problema, una idea, otra cosa) y, solo si lo escribís, tu email, a feedback.jurisgames.com, que lo envía por email a hello@jurisgames.com sin guardar nada. Con "Incluir detalles técnicos" (tildado al principio; destildalo para no mandarlos, y "Ver lo que se envía" muestra todo antes de enviarlo) también manda tu navegador y sistema, tamaño de pantalla y de ventana, idioma, tipo de conexión, errores recientes de la página y en qué página estás. Nunca la lista, sus links ni el nombre de nadie. El email queda en nuestra casilla lo necesario para responder y arreglar las cosas. Para frenar abusos, el servidor cuenta los mensajes por red y por día con un código irreversible de la dirección (nunca la dirección), que se borra al día siguiente. Contacto: hello@jurisgames.com
